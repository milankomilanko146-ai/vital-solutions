Quick answers

Does it burn like chili creams?

It starts cold, then warmth builds. Capsicum can feel stronger on sensitive skin, so start with a thin layer.

Is it a medicine? Is it safe?

It’s a cosmetic product for external use, not a medicine. Don’t use on broken skin, near eyes, or if allergic to any ingredient (contains methyl salicylate). Wash hands after use. If pregnant, nursing or under treatment, ask your doctor first.
